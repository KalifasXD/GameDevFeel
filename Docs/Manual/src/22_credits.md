# Credits and licenses {#ch22}

FeelKit's code, recipes, materials and comfort menu are made for FeelKit and covered by its Fab license. The sample sounds come from other authors, all under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 (public domain): they can be used, changed and shipped in a commercial game, and no credit is required. They are credited here anyway, and in `Credits.md` in the plugin folder.

## FeelKit's license {#ch22_license}

Both editions are distributed on Fab under Fab's Standard License. FeelKit Lite is free; FeelKit Pro is sold with Personal and Professional licenses. The license terms are Fab's, linked from the listings. FeelKit contains no third-party code and no fonts.

## Demo projects {#ch22_demos}

The playable demos and their source projects are built on Epic's Unreal Engine templates, whose content is covered by Epic's license for Unreal Engine. The Action/RPG demo's own sounds and its guard shield material are listed in the `Credits.md` of that demo project.

## Your own project {#ch22_project}

Nothing here has to stay in a game. A library recipe copied into a project points at these samples, and its sounds and materials can be swapped for the game's own without changing anything else.
