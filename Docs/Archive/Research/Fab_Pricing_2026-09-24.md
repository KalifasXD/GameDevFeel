- Professional licences are **1.5 to 2 times** the Personal price.
- Nobody else offers a free edition. FeelKit Lite lets buyers try the editor and the comfort layer first, which lowers
  the risk of paying a little more for Pro.
- FeelKit starts with no reviews. The first reviews decide how fast it sells, so the launch price should make the first
  purchases easy.
- Fab takes 12%: $34.99 leaves $30.79 per sale, $39.99 leaves $35.19. Twenty sales a month is $616 against $704.
- Fab rules: no discount in the first 30 days after publishing (1.7.1.c); the base price must not be raised just to
  advertise a bigger discount later (1.7.2.g).
